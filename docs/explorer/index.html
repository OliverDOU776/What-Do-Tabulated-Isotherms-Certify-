Why this matters. Mixture models read a pure-gas isotherm far outside its measured window. A fit that matches the window says little about that region; the certified band states the range that the window can support.

Data & scope. Records are a random sample of the paper's held-out test set (records measured up to at most 10 bar). Bands and fits were computed from the visible points only. A hidden point counts as covered when it lies inside the band widened by the record's tolerance. In the paper, the Langmuir-site band covers 97.0% of 12,402 hidden points; coverage drops above 10 bar.

    Data & scope. Real ISODB pairs measured to 5–40 bar; the explorer withholds the points above 1 bar and returns them as you move the sliders. After each extension the record is re-admitted and compiled with the standard protocol, so the interval is not forced to shrink.
